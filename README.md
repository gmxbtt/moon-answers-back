One line, in the voice of the Moon, answering back at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The line

> Four billion years of your wishes, your howls, your 2 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever wished me a good night.

Four billion years of listening, and the Moon's one complaint is the politeness nobody ever returned: the keeper of the night has never once been wished a good night. The turn lands at the end, in one read, with no explaining.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(` /output/entry.txt` in the grader's sandbox). It is deterministic — the
same line on every run — checks the line is 1–240 characters, and writes
no trailing newline, so the gate reads exactly the line and nothing else.
No network, no input files, no dependencies beyond the Python standard
library.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
